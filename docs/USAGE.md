# Using voicebridge

Talk to Claude Code, and hear it talk back, at your desk or from your phone.
This is the quick guide to the modes and the everyday controls. It is for Claude
Code today, and it is built to work with other agents (Codex, Antigravity, and
more) next.

## Turn it on

Inside any Claude Code session:

- `/voice-on` , replies are read aloud; you type, or hold space to talk.
- `/voice-agent` , hands-free. Everything you say goes to Claude, replies come
  back out loud, and you can talk over a reply to change direction.
- `/voice-wake` , it stays quiet and only listens when you say "hey Claude", so
  you can talk to people in the room without it jumping in.
- `/voice-phone` , shows a QR code. Scan it and your phone becomes a remote for
  your computer. Talk to your live session from anywhere in the house.
- `/voice-off` , stop.

## While you are talking

- **Interrupt** just by talking over it, it stops and takes your new direction.
- **Speed it up or slow it down** if it is talking too fast or too slow.
- **Mute** to stop it listening for a moment; **Replay** to hear the last reply
  again if you missed it.
- **Stop** to cancel what it is working on.
- **Approve by voice**, when it needs a yes or no, just say yes or no.

## Running several projects at once

- Ask "which agents need me?" and it tells you.
- Say "switch to <name>" to move your voice to another project.
- It speaks up on its own the moment one of them finishes and needs you.

## From your phone

Run `/voice-phone`, scan the QR, tap Start, and talk. Your Mac keeps doing the
work; your phone is just the call, and it speaks the replies back. Keep the Mac
awake and the session focused.

## Tips

- Pause naturally. It waits for you to finish before it sends what you said.
- If you are in a quiet room, type in the composer or use wake-word mode.
- Full list of commands and hotkeys: `/voice-help`.
